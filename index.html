📋 Quick Start Guide

📸

Supported Images

Upload a time-series of germination plate photos. The pipeline works best with:

  • Standard digital camera photos (JPEG, PNG)
  • Microscopy captures (TIFF, 8- or 16-bit)
  • Web formats (WEBP)
  • Top-down photos of seeds on filter paper or agar
  • Consistent lighting and camera position between frames
📁

Upload Methods

Four ways to load your data:

  • Individual files — select or drag multiple images
  • ZIP archive — drop a .zip containing your image series
  • Time-lapse video — drop a single video file and choose how many evenly-spaced frames to extract
  • Demo dataset — click below to load a sample Arabidopsis time-series

Files are sorted by name (use zero-padded numbering, e.g. frame_001.jpg or timestamps like 041224_1430.jpg).

🔬

How It Works

Germinator AI uses a multi-stage pipeline based on Joosen et al. (2010):

  • Segment — excess-blue contrast separates seeds from background
  • Calibrate — t₀ seed median defines the scale ruler
  • Register — centroid voting aligns frames
  • Track — geodesic BFS assigns each pixel to a seed identity
  • Score — absorbing-state model detects germination onset
📊

Output & Metrics

After analysis you get:

  • Gmax — maximum germination percentage
  • t₅₀ — time to 50% germination
  • U₈₄₋₁₆ — germination uniformity
  • AUC — area under curve
  • Per-seed CSV export and full JSON report
  • Interactive seed overlay map with germination state
  • Hill-fit 4-parameter dose-response curve

📚 Open benchmark datasets for validation

These open-access datasets can be used to validate Germinator AI against published results:

  • SPIRO Assays — Arabidopsis time-lapse on agar plates, hourly captures (Ohlsson et al. 2024, CC BY 4.0)
  • ChronoRoot 1.0 — Arabidopsis every 15 min over 17 days (GigaDB, CC0)
  • SeedGerm-VIG — Wheat/Barley/Rice multi-phase annotations (BioImage Archive, CC0)
  • Genze et al. 2020 — Maize/Rye 23,000+ images with bounding boxes (Mendeley Data, CC BY 4.0)
